# Meet the SO-101 hardware

SO-101 is an open desktop arm design used for robotics and imitation learning. The follower performs the task; the leader measures the operator's motion to produce control targets. Kits with the same name can differ in power, printed parts, cameras and boards.

## Five axes or six?

There are five rotary joints in the arm chain plus a gripper actuator. **Five arm axes plus gripper** explains why six action channels are expected without implying arbitrary six-dimensional tip pose control.

| Channel | Common name | Visible motion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `shoulder_pan` | Base left/right rotation |
| 2 | `shoulder_lift` | Upper arm up/down |
| 3 | `elbow_flex` | Elbow bending |
| 4 | `wrist_flex` | Wrist bending |
| 5 | `wrist_roll` | Wrist axial rotation |
| 6 | `gripper` | Jaw opening/closing |

These are not an automatic index mapping. Simulation can expose names `1`–`6`; hardware uses other names. Derive the real ordering from metadata and the robot API.

## The supplier page you provided

The [Hashtag Robotics page](https://labs.hashtagrobotics.tr/so-101-robot-kol) lists assembled/tested leader and follower, STS3215 servos, USB-C and camera add-ons. Advertised reach/weight figures do not replace measuring your delivered workspace or load behavior. Do not assume separately offered cameras are included.

The advertised **30 kg·cm** is servo torque, not a 30 kg payload. Torque is approximately force multiplied by lever length; the same object creates a larger shoulder moment when extended horizontally. Joint torque, temperature, printing, connections and dynamic motion all affect load capacity. One servo figure cannot establish reliable arm payload.

Follower and leader supply details differ. Verify the actual adapter and motor variant labels rather than assigning a voltage from the STS3215 name alone. Follow the delivered cable/adapter mapping and manufacturer instructions.

The official description specifies six 1/345 follower motors and joint-dependent leader ratios. Leader feel therefore should not be equated with follower dynamics. [LeRobot SO-101](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/en/so101)

  front: MODEL_SERIAL_PLACEMENT
  wrist: MODEL_SERIAL_PLACEMENT

Keep it with photos, calibration files and package versions. When rebuilding the setup, identify `front` from the card rather than memory.

## Your first workspace

Choose a small clearly visible desk area. Mount the arm, keep power cutoff accessible and route cables clear of joint travel. Use a light, visible, unbreakable first object. Verify movement direction and control before exploring capacity limits.

The reachable region is larger than the reliably usable task region. Exclude positions hidden from the camera, near limits or requiring an unsuitable approach. Test a few empty-gripper targets first.
